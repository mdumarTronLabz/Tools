The 10 Patient Touchpoints Every Clinic Needs

Patients expect to find these before they ever pick up the phone. Check which ones your clinic currently offers online.

☐
1. A Professional Website Not just social media. A dedicated site that shows you are established and trustworthy.
☐
2. Fast Mobile Experience Patients search on phones. If your page (or Facebook link) loads slowly, they leave.
☐
3. Instant WhatsApp / Booking Patients expect to message or book in under 90 seconds. Phone-only contact loses them.
☐
4. Doctor Profiles & Photos Patients choose the doctor, not just the clinic. They want to see who will treat them.
☐
5. Treatment-Specific Pages Generic "Services" pages don't rank. Each treatment needs its own detailed page.
☐
6. Visible Reviews & Proof Google reviews, before/after photos, and patient stories build instant credibility.
☐
7. Local Search Presence When patients search "dentist near me" or "skin clinic in [city]," can they find you?
☐
8. Answers to Common Questions FAQ pages help voice search (Alexa, Google) recommend your clinic over others.
☐
9. Local Language Support Patients trust clinics that speak their language. Hindi, French, Spanish, Danish, Arabic etc.
☐
10. Security & Privacy Signals SSL, privacy policies, and secure forms. Patients share health data — they need to feel safe.
