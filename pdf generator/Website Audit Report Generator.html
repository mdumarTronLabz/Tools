Top 5 Patient Leaks

These are the specific issues actively costing you patient enquiries. Each leak has a measurable impact on your conversion rate and revenue.

⚠️ Patient Leak #1: Slow Mobile Loading
Where:
Homepage, all mobile pages
Patient Effect:
Patient searches your clinic, clicks your site, waits 9+ seconds, leaves before seeing services
Impact:
53% of mobile visitors abandon sites that take over 3 seconds to load
Fix: Compress images, enable lazy loading, switch to faster hosting, implement CDN
Effort:
3–5 days
⚠️ Patient Leak #2: No WhatsApp Booking Button
Where:
Mobile homepage, treatment pages
Patient Effect:
Patient ready to book, can't find easy contact method, closes tab
Impact:
67% of Indian patients prefer WhatsApp for clinic booking. Missing button = 40%+ conversion loss
Fix: Add sticky WhatsApp button with pre-filled message: "Hi, I'd like to book an appointment for [treatment]"
Effort:
1 day
⚠️ Patient Leak #3: Hidden Doctor Credentials
Where:
Doctor info buried in "About Us" page
Patient Effect:
Patient can't verify doctor qualifications immediately, loses trust, leaves to find another clinic
Impact:
No doctor visibility = 30-40% lower conversion to booking
Fix: Add doctor profile card on homepage + each treatment page with: Photo, Name, Qualifications, Registration #, Experience, Testimonial
Effort:
2–3 days
⚠️ Patient Leak #4: No Treatment-Specific Pages
Where:
Generic "Services" page instead of individual treatment pages
Patient Effect:
Patient searching "dental implants in [city]" can't find you, finds competitor instead
Impact:
Weak SEO + generic content = invisible in Google searches + low conversion
Fix: Create dedicated pages for top 8-12 treatments with: Problem description, Solution, Before/After photos, FAQ, Pricing range, CTA
Effort:
2–3 weeks
⚠️ Patient Leak #5: No Google Reviews Visible
Where:
Homepage, footer, contact page
Patient Effect:
Patient can't see proof of quality, checks Google directly, loses confidence in website
Impact:
92% of patients trust online reviews. Hidden reviews = immediate trust loss
Fix: Embed Google reviews widget on homepage + testimonial section. Add "Leave us a review" CTA
Effort:
1 day
